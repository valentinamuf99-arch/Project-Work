## The concept

Own-train warning tells a commuter who takes the same train every day, without them having to check, that this train is late or cancelled, and why. It is for the office-day commuter whose first train starts upstream at Bassano and may already have built up delay before he leaves home at about six (P2, F1) `[thin: the hour is an observation in the note, not a quote]`; for the pass holder who wants to know, before going to the station, when her own train will reach her stop (P3, F8); and for the student who finds her usual train late at Padova station after her lesson (P6, F9) `[thin: P6 uses no app; see Assumptions]`. The commuter names their usual train once (41) and sets the delay that would change their plans (3); the service then speaks only when that delay is passed or the train is cancelled (1). The warning gives the cause (8), lets the commuter see where the train is on its way (5), and comes with the next workable way to make the trip (11) `[thin: rests on P2 and P6 in the moment, with P1 and P4 outside it; no feature in the set supplies it]`. The warning gives the facts; the decision stays with the commuter, who keeps their own threshold, as P2 decides on his ("se vedo che mezz'ora di ritardo, magari neanche vado in stazione", Pt.2 [00:06:15]). It works at home before leaving (TP 10), in the minutes before departure, where the operator's word today comes too late (TP 11 `(?)`, TP 44), and at the station up to boarding (TP 23) `[thin: at the station it rests on P6, who reads the board, not a warning]`. The moment is the same day, from home up to the platform, before boarding.

The quotes the paragraph rests on:

> "Magari 1º di andare in stazione controllo se il treno un ritardo effettivamente. Il mio parte da Bassano, fa in tempo ad accumulare un tot di ritardo e quindi, se vedo che mezz'ora di ritardo, magari neanche vado in stazione o cerco un modo alternativo o chiedo a qualcuno, eccetera." — P2 Pt.2 [00:06:15]

> "puoi inserire il numero del tuo treno e ti dà aggiornamenti in tempo reale" — P3 [00:14:51]

> "Capire se è anche una cosa magari temporanea o che si può eventualmente prolungare e quindi dopo creare un ritardo più ampio." — P6 [00:11:04]

> "può essere che ti avvisi proprio pochi minuti 1º" — P3 [00:14:29] (TP 11, marked (?): push or app status, not settled)

## Beyond the moment

Not part of the prototype and not in the feature set.

## Assumptions

What the concept takes for granted without evidence, and what would test it.

- **P6 would use a working app.** Marked as an assumption by the author. P6 uses no app ([00:02:25] "Non non utilizzo app"); she tried it once and left it after bugs, "non ci sono motivi particolari" ([00:12:12]); she expects the reason at the station because she has no other way to get it ([00:11:38]). Test: show P6 the warning flow and ask whether she would receive it on her phone, and what would make her drop it again.
- **The cause helps the decision before boarding.** Concept 8 is V `[weak]`: P2 decides on minutes, not causes. P6's "so that" is judging whether the delay will grow; P4's is telling classmates or work (`notifying-others`), not deciding. Test: show P2 and P6 the same warning with and without the cause and ask what each would do.
- **A cause is known when the warning is sent.** P1 never learned the reason ([00:10:42]); P4 learned it from the conductor after an hour and a half ([00:06:18]). Nothing in the material says the cause exists early enough to go into a warning before leaving home. Test: the open-data check still listed as never done in the gaps note.
- **The delay is known before the commuter leaves.** F1's own circumstance is a train that builds delay on its way (Pt.2 [00:06:15]); TP 11 and TP 44 show the word arriving minutes ahead or late. Test: the same open-data check, for one early train from Bassano across several mornings.
- **A commuter sets a threshold.** Only P2 states one ("mezz'ora", Pt.2 [00:06:15]). P6 calls ten minutes not excessive ([00:06:24]), which is a judgement, not a set number. Test: ask P3, P5 and P6 at what delay they would change plans, and whether they would set it in advance.
- **There is one usual train to save.** P2, P3 and P6 speak of their own train; P4 takes the direct train or the one with a change at Castelfranco, depending on her timetable (P4 note, unit on her route). Test: ask each participant how many different trains they take in a week.
- **There is a workable other way to show.** Concept 11 needs one; no feature in the set produces it, and P2's own "other way" includes asking someone ("chiedo a qualcuno"). On his own route P2 says there is none and does not look for one (Pt.2.1 [00:01:34] "non c'è un'alternativa, io so che non c'è un'alternativa e di conseguenza non la cerco"); he misses a proposed alternative only in a place he does not know (Pt.2.1 [00:07:50]). The point this raises: the alternative may matter most on an unfamiliar route, not on the usual one the concept saves. Test: ask P2 what he actually did on the last morning the first train was late.
