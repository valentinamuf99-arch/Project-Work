# Possible solutions · twelve concepts per question, #1 and #4

## Scope and method

Two questions from `2026-10-26-how-might-we-ai.md` were answered with twelve concepts each, twenty-four in total. The goal is range, not choice: nothing here is ranked or recommended. This note replaces nothing; `2026-10-26-ideation-ai.md` stays as written.

- **HMW 1 · #1 · F1 · P2.** HMW let an early commuter know, before leaving home at six, that their first train is already late or cancelled, so they can choose whether to go to the station at all?
- **HMW 2 · #4 · F4 · P4.** HMW make sure an evening student learns from the service, days ahead, that a strike hits their route and which of their trains still run?

Each concept says what it does in one line, not how. A direction is a family of answers, not a variation of one answer. A concept is marked `[risky]` when it rests on an assumption that F1, F4 and their quotes do not support. `[by hand]` marks a concept that matches one in the author's list in `2026-10-26-ideation-human.md`.

## Directions

- **A · Own-train word.** The fact about the person's own train reaches them before they move. Concepts 1, 2, 13.
- **B · People carry it.** Other riders, staff or classmates carry the warning, not the service's systems. Concepts 7, 18, 19.
- **C · Personal threshold.** The person sets how bad it must be before they hear about it. Concept 3.
- **D · Fallback ready.** The warning arrives with what to do instead. Concepts 4, 5, 16, 17.
- **E · Shrink the uncertainty.** The service makes the part nobody knows smaller or more honest. Concepts 6, 15.
- **F · Lower the stakes.** Work or the university bends, so a failed train matters less. Concepts 9, 21.
- **G · Change the service.** The train service changes so the situation behind the question happens less. Concepts 10, 22.
- **H · Service carries the cost.** The service pays back what the failure costs the person. Concepts 11, 23.
- **I · Service acts.** The service takes the step for the person, so the decision disappears. Concepts 12, 24.
- **J · Tell the others.** The warning comes with the word to the people who wait for them. Concepts 8, 20.
- **K · Rule made personal.** A rule that exists in general is read out for this person's trains. Concept 14.

Each question reaches ten of the eleven directions: HMW 1 has no K, HMW 2 has no C. The four by-hand concepts sit in A, B and E only.

## Why the risky concepts are marked

- **A likelihood is useful (6).** P2 asks for the fact, how late the train is: "se vedo che mezz'ora di ritardo, magari neanche vado in stazione" (Pt.2 [00:06:15]). No quote asks for odds.
- **Other riders or classmates carry the warning (7, 19).** Neither F1 nor F4 mentions fellow riders as a source. P4 heard of the strike from the conductor, staff not peers ([00:11:38]); that is why concept 18 is not marked.
- **Work or the university adapts (9, 21).** P2 says he can skip work and that "tanti altri no" (Pt.2.1 [00:01:17]); the stake for his workplace is not in F1. F4 says nothing about lessons.
- **Money is the cost that matters (11, 23).** P2 mentions lost money only for missed connections (Pt.2.1 [00:02:58]), not for staying home. F4 does not mention money.
- **The person accepts a step taken for them (12, 24).** No quote supports it, and it pulls against the `autonomy` theme in the plan: P4 read the rule and chose her own trains ([00:12:01], [00:12:23]).
- **Family is involved on a strike day (20).** P4 calls her parents in another story (F12, a stalled train), not in the strike story.

Concepts 5 and 8 are not marked: P2 names a lift to another station for the second train (Pt.2.1 [00:01:34]) and writing to work that he will be late or not come (Pt.2.1 [00:00:48]).

## Dropped concepts

These came up but answer a different question, so they were dropped, not defended.

- **Cancelled or only late, in the limbo.** It answers #2 (P2 at half past nine, train neither departed nor cancelled), not the check before leaving home.
- **Disruptions filtered to one's route.** It answers #5 (the network-wide banner), and returns to the form F5 names.
- **Strike in the news, found faster.** It improves P2's Google and Telegram search (Pt.2.1 [00:11:02]), but HMW 2 asks that the student learn it from the service.
- **Pass valid on the replacement bus.** It is strike-related but answers #14 (P3's ticket), not which trains still run.
- **Refund for a strike-day ticket.** It answers cost after the fact, not learning days ahead.
- **Ask the service whether my train runs.** It leaves the student to think of asking; HMW 2 says "make sure" they learn it.

## Signals

This unit is a reading of the concepts, not what the sources say.

- **The by-hand list sits in three directions, all about the fact itself.** Push alert, live position, odds and riders up the line all deliver the state of the train. D, J and C, the directions with the most quotes behind them for HMW 1 (Pt.2.1 [00:07:50] "manca la soluzione alternativa proposta"), are new.
- **HMW 2 has evidence for a direction HMW 1 lacks.** P4 already had the information, as a rule on the website, and did the reading herself ([00:12:01]). K and concepts 15 to 17 follow her own steps.
- **G sidesteps both questions.** Concepts 10 and 22 remove the situation rather than answer it; they are kept because each still names the question's moment.
