# Touchpoints · every moment of contact between commuter and service

## Scope and method

A touchpoint here is one specific moment in which a commuter is in contact with the service: an app, a website, a board, a train, a ticket counter or validator, an announcement, a member of staff. It is not a category and not a solution.

Every note listed in `index.md` under `research/Notes/` and `research/Desk research/` was read. The transcripts were not read: the notes carry the quotes with their timestamps. `touchpoints-human.md` was not read before writing, so this list is independent of it.

Rules applied: only what the notes say; every row cites its note and a quote that shows the moment; the same moment in several notes is one row with several citations; ambiguous moments stay in, marked (?), with the quote that makes them ambiguous; conflicts between notes are kept and marked ⚠.

## Contradictions

Notes that describe the same moment in conflicting ways. Both sides are kept in the tables above.

- **C1, is the board reliable?** P3 says the board always shows "ritardo, cancellato" in large letters [00:15:29], and P6 relies on it alone. P1 says the board showed her train as on time when it was at least 15 min late [00:09:00]. The source participants trust most failed at the moment that mattered.
- **C2, announcements during a stop.** P5 says the loudspeakers announce a restart time [00:07:51]. P4 says there was nothing from the speaker or the conductor for half an hour [00:14:57, 00:16:22]. This could be a different line or train; neither note settles it.
- **C3, is there staff to ask?** P1 has seen a conductor about three times since 2021 [00:19:03]. For P3, P4 and P6, staff are the only working source: the conductor holds a connection, gives the cause, and staff are asked at the station.
- **C4, when do staff act?** The P3 conductor acts ahead to save the connection [00:17:41]. P6 says staff "non ti anticipano nulla" and act only after the problem [00:14:01].
- **C5, benchmark vs interviews.** The benchmark note's "People outside the apps" bullet says P2 and P3 open an app only to buy tickets. Row 10 shows both check before leaving home. The benchmark note already flags this.
- **C6, benchmark vs P1.** The benchmark's "Understanding" bullet says P1 got the cause from people. The P1 note records that no staff were there and that she never learned the reason [00:10:42]. The benchmark note already flags this.

## Signals

This unit is a reading of the tables, not what the sources say.

- **The board is the shared touchpoint.** Five of six participants read it (rows 22 to 26), including P6, who uses no app. It is also the touchpoint that failed P1 (C1).
- **Staff are the only source of a cause.** Every cause the operator gave, and the one held connection, came from a person (rows 41, 42), but whether a person is there varies (C3, C4).
- **The app is mostly a ticket wallet.** Most app rows are about buying, validating or showing a ticket (rows 1, 7, 33, 34). Where it is used for information, the notes record it as late, generic or silent (rows 11, 13, 16, 17, 40, 44).
