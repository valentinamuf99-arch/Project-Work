# Brief 01 · Redesigning the commute

**Who is the user?**
A regular commuter who travels the same home–work route by public transport and knows it well, including its usual delays.

**What is the core problem?**
They cannot tell when today is different: when the routine breaks, because of a cancelled run, a strike or a missed connection, they decide late and with partial information, often while already waiting at the stop.

**Why does this problem interest me?**
I once waited an hour in Rome for a bus that never came and found a way home only by chance, and I realised that the hardest part was not the delay but having to decide with no information.
