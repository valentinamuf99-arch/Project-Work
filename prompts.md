Constraints:
- 30 minutes, remote. Add an approximate time for each section.
- Participants are commuters in the Vicenza–Padova area, by train or bus.
- Write everything in English, including the questions.

Structure:
1. Intro: purpose, consent to record, no right or wrong answers (2 min)
2. Warm-up: who they are, their daily commute (3 min)
3. The routine: a normal day, step by step, including which apps they use (5 min)
4. When the routine breaks: ask them to tell the last time something went
   wrong (delay, cancellation, strike, missed connection). What happened,
   how they found out, what they decided, how they felt (10 min)
5. Information and tools: where they look for information, what they trust,
   what is missing (5 min)
6. Attitude towards help: how they would feel about being warned or advised,
   without describing any solution or mentioning AI first (3 min)
7. Wrap-up: anything else, thanks (2 min)

Rules:
- Ask about real past episodes, not hypothetical situations.
- No leading questions and no yes/no questions.
- Add 1–2 follow-up probes for the key questions.
