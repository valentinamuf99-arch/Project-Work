# Possible solutions · twelve concepts per question

## Scope and method

Three questions from `2026-10-26-how-might-we-ai.md` were answered with twelve concepts each, thirty-six in total. The goal is range, not choice: nothing here is ranked or recommended.

- **#1** HMW let an early commuter know, before leaving home at six, that their first train is already late or cancelled, so they can choose whether to go to the station at all?
- **#4** HMW make sure an evening student learns from the service, days ahead, that a strike hits their route and which of their trains still run?
- **#3** HMW help a commuter make a plan in the minutes when the fate of their train is still unclear or cancelled? This wording adds "or cancelled" to the note's version, which reads "still unclear" only.

Each concept says what it does in one line, not how. A direction is a family of solutions, not a variation of one idea. A concept is marked `[risky]` when it rests on an assumption no quote in the HMW note supports.

Count: 36 concepts, 12 per question, 8 directions, 20 marked `[risky]` (7 for #1, 5 for #4, 8 for #3).

## Directions

- **A · Earlier word.** The fact reaches the person before they have to move. Concepts 1, 5, 6, 13, 17.
- **B · People as channel.** Staff, fellow riders, classmates or the university carry the information or the help, not the service's systems. Concepts 2, 7, 14, 15, 20, 21, 28, 33.
- **C · Service decides.** The service acts or chooses on the commuter's behalf, so the decision disappears. Concepts 4, 18, 31, 34.
- **D · Lower the stakes.** Obligations outside the train (work, lessons) bend, so a failed train matters less. Concepts 3, 11, 16, 35.
- **E · Plan before it fails.** The fallback already exists when the train fails. Concepts 8, 19, 26, 30.
- **F · Shrink the uncertainty.** The service makes the time spent not knowing shorter, fixed or more honest. Concepts 9, 22, 25, 27, 29.
- **G · Change the service.** The train service itself changes so the situation behind the question happens less. Concepts 10, 23, 32.
- **H · Service carries the cost.** The service pays back what the failure costs the commuter. Concepts 12, 24, 36.

The spread is uneven: B holds eight concepts, G and H three each. Each question reaches at least seven of the eight directions.

## Why the risky concepts are marked

- **Other riders or groups help (2, 7, 15, 20, 28, 33).** No quote in the HMW note says commuters want help or information from other riders or classmates.
- **A workplace, school or university adapts (3, 11, 16, 21, 35).** P2's "magari neanche vado in stazione" (Pt.2 [00:06:15]) implies not going is possible, but says nothing about the employer. P4 says nothing about the school.
- **The commuter accepts a choice made for them (4, 5, 31, 34).** No quote supports it, and it may conflict with the `autonomy` theme in the plan.
- **Money is the cost that matters (12, 24, 36).** Neither P2 nor P4 speaks of money in these quotes; the only money story (P3, F15) belongs to another question.
- **A likelihood is useful (27).** P2 asks for the fact, cancelled or late (Pt.2.1 [00:14:23]), not for odds.
- **Commuters plan ahead (30).** No story mentions a plan prepared before the failure.

## Discarded concepts

These came up but answer a different question, so they were dropped, not defended.

- **Stop-by-stop live position.** It answers #7 (P3's arrival time at their stop), not going or not at six, the strike, or planning under uncertainty.
- **Calming announcement on a stopped train.** It answers #9 (P1 on board), a moment none of the three questions covers.
- **Route-filtered disruption banner.** It answers #5, and it returns to a form from the story ("filtered") rather than to the strike question.
- **Pass check for the replacement bus.** It is strike-related but answers #14 (P3's ticket), not learning days ahead which trains run.
- **Parents warned automatically.** It answers #10 and #11 (P4 calling home from a stalled train), not the strike notice days ahead.

## Signals

This unit is a reading of the concepts, not what the sources say.

- **Only directions A and F stay close to the evidence.** Both deliver the fact P2 and P4 named. Every concept in B, D and H, and most in C, adds an actor, an agreement or a payment that the stories do not mention.
- **The strike question is the only one where people already acted as the channel.** P4 heard from the conductor (concept 14 moves that earlier); the other questions have no such precedent.
- **Direction G sidesteps the question.** Concepts 10, 23 and 32 remove the situation rather than answer it; they are kept because each still names the question's moment.
