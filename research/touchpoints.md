# Touchpoints

Touchpoints identified from the research: the moments of interaction between the user and the service.

---

## 1. Planning and information

| # | Touchpoint | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| TP-01 | Checking for strikes or other information in the app | App |
| TP-02 | Checking for strikes or other information on the website | Website |
| TP-03 | Checking the website for real-time information on the relevant train | Website |
| TP-04 | Checking the app for the train's real-time status | App |

## 2. Ticket purchase and payment

| # | Touchpoint | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| TP-05 | Using the app to buy tickets | App |
| TP-06 | Using the website to buy a season pass | Website |
| TP-07 | Using the app to buy bus tickets | App |
| TP-08 | Paying for tickets by card | Payment |
| TP-09 | Trying to buy a ticket 5 minutes before departure | App / Station |
| TP-10 | Buying a ticket via Tap&Tap at the station | Station (contactless) |
| TP-11 | Using the app to validate tickets | App |

## 3. At the station

| # | Touchpoint | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| TP-12 | Checking the train time in the app on arrival at the station | App |
| TP-13 | Checking the station screens for times, delays and cancellations | Station displays |
| TP-14 | Listening to station announcements | Station PA system |
| TP-15 | Asking the train conductor or information desk about times, delays or cancellations | Staff |

## 4. On board

| # | Touchpoint | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| TP-16 | Using the app during the journey to show the ticket to the inspector | App |

## 5. Disruptions and alternatives

| # | Touchpoint | Channel |
|---|---|---|
